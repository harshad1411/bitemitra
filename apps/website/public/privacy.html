Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Jamzo collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what your rights are. It applies to the Jamzo app for customers, the Jamzo Restaurant Partner app, the Jamzo Delivery Partner app, jamzo.in and Jamzo Admin.

Jamzo is operated by JAMZO ("Jamzo", "we", "us"), 64, Agharaparu, Block A, near Umiyamata Road, Unjha, District Mahesana, Gujarat 384170, India. For any privacy question or complaint, contact our Grievance Officer (section 10).

We process personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

3. Where your data is stored

Our servers and database are in India (Bangalore). Photos and uploaded documents are stored with our hosting provider in Singapore and delivered through a content network.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account — while you use Jamzo. If you ask us to delete it, we remove your name, mobile number, email and saved addresses within 30 days.
  • Orders, payments, invoices and accounts — as long as tax and accounting laws require (generally up to 8 years), with your identity removed after deletion.
  • Delivery partners' location history — 90 days, then deleted.
  • Sign-in codes — expire after 5 minutes.

5. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • access a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it (most details can be changed in the app);
  • delete your account in the app (customers: Account → Delete my account; partners: at the bottom of the home screen): you are signed out at once and the data above is erased after 30 days, unless you sign in and keep the account before then. Delivery partners first need no cash in hand and no unpaid earnings; the only owner of a restaurant first asks Jamzo to add another owner or close the restaurant;
  • withdraw consent (for example, location in your phone's settings, or notifications);
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act;
  • complain to our Grievance Officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

We answer requests within 30 days.

6. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS); bank details are encrypted in our database; staff access is limited by role and logged; sign-in uses one-time codes, and Jamzo Admin also requires a code sent by email.

7. Children

Jamzo is for people aged 18 and over. Customers under 18 may use it only with a parent or guardian.

8. Cookies

Our websites use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

9. Changes

We will show changes in the app before they take effect.
