# Delivery Partner Terms

**DRAFT for legal review — not yet in force.** Accepted in the Jamzo Delivery Partner app when applying.
[[REVIEW: independent-contractor wording and gig-worker obligations under the Code on Social Security, 2020.]]

Between **[[LEGAL NAME]]** ("Jamzo") and you, the delivery partner.

## 1. Your relationship with Jamzo
You are an **independent delivery partner**, not an employee. You choose when to go online and which orders to
accept. You use your own vehicle and phone.

## 2. Joining
- You must be **18 or older**, hold a valid **driving licence** (for motor vehicles), **vehicle registration** and
  **insurance**, and give true documents. Bicycle partners need [[ID proof and PAN]].
- Signing in does not make you a partner: Jamzo checks your documents and **approves** you first.
- Keep your documents up to date in the app.

## 3. Deliveries
- When you are online, Jamzo may **offer** you orders. You see the pickup, the drop area and your **estimated pay**
  before you accept.
- Collect the order only after checking the **order number**; deliver to the customer's address; ask for the
  **delivery code** when the app asks for it.
- Be polite and safe: follow traffic laws, wear a helmet, never ride under the influence.
- Do not open, tamper with or consume food.
- Do not contact customers outside the app or keep their details.

## 4. Pay
- For each completed delivery you earn the **trip pay shown in the app** ([[base pay + distance pay, with
  incentives where offered]]). The final amount appears in Earnings after delivery.
- **Tips are 100% yours.**
- If a trip is cancelled after you were assigned, you are paid [[the trip pay estimate]] as the app shows.
- Jamzo pays your earnings **[[weekly]]** to your verified bank account or UPI ID, with a statement. Amounts below
  [[₹100]] carry over.

## 5. Cash on delivery
- For cash orders you collect the exact amount shown and keep it safely.
- Cash you hold is shown in the app. You must **deposit it** to Jamzo as the app shows (UPI or bank) within
  [[24 hours]] or before your limit of [[₹5,000]]; above the limit, cash orders stop until you deposit.
- Cash may be **netted against your earnings** at settlement.

## 6. Location and data
While you are **online or on a trip**, the app shares your location with Jamzo about every 10 seconds, to offer you
nearby orders and to show customers where their order is (rounded to about 100 m). It is not used when you are
offline. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

## 7. Ratings and conduct
Customers can rate your delivery; you see your own average. Jamzo may **pause or remove** a partner for fraud,
unsafe driving, missing cash deposits, repeated complaints or abuse, and will tell you the reason.

## 8. Ending
You can stop using the app at any time. Earnings owed are paid at the next settlement, less any cash you still
hold.

Indian law applies; courts at [[Mehsana, Gujarat]]. Contact: [[partners@jamzo.in]].
