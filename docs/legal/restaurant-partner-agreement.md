## 1. What Jamzo does
Jamzo lists the Restaurant on its customer app, takes orders and payments from customers, arranges delivery by
independent delivery partners, handles customer support, and pays the Restaurant its share.

## 2. The Restaurant's responsibilities
1. Hold a valid **FSSAI licence** and all local licences, and keep them current in the partner app.
2. Prepare food that is **safe, hygienic, as described**, correctly labelled **veg / non-veg / egg**, and packed
   so it travels well and is tamper-evident.
3. Keep the **menu, prices, photos and availability** accurate (mark items sold out; pause when busy or closed).
4. **Accept or decline orders promptly** — within [[3 minutes]] — and keep to the preparation time it sets.
5. Hand orders only to the assigned delivery partner (check the order number).
6. Not share or misuse customer details (the Restaurant sees only first names and order details).

## 3. Prices and commission
- The Restaurant sets its own **menu prices** ("Restaurant prices").
- Customers may see **Jamzo prices**, which can be higher than Restaurant prices; the difference belongs to Jamzo
  and is not part of the Restaurant's earnings. [[REVIEW Q-4.]]
- Jamzo charges a **commission of [[__%]]** on [[the Restaurant price of food sold, after discounts the Restaurant
  funds]], plus GST on the commission.
- **Discounts the Restaurant chooses to fund** are deducted from its earnings; discounts Jamzo funds are not.
- Packaging charges set for the Restaurant are passed through to it.

## 4. Payouts
- Jamzo settles **[[weekly, every Monday]]** for delivered orders in the previous period, by bank transfer to the
  Restaurant's verified account. Amounts below [[₹100]] carry over to the next settlement.
- Each settlement comes with a **statement** (orders, sales, commission, taxes, discounts, refunds, adjustments),
  also visible in the partner app.
- Taxes Jamzo must withhold by law (such as **TCS under GST** and **TDS under the Income Tax Act**) are deducted and
  shown on the statement. [[REVIEW Q-3.]]

## 5. Cancellations and refunds
- If a **customer cancels after the Restaurant accepted**, the Restaurant is still paid its food value.
- If the **Restaurant cancels or fails to accept**, the customer is refunded in full and the Restaurant earns
  nothing for that order; repeated cancellations may lead to temporary removal.
- If a customer complaint is caused by the Restaurant (missing or wrong items, quality), the refund may be
  recovered from the Restaurant's earnings, with the reason shown on the statement.

## 6. Ratings
Customers rate items after delivery. The Restaurant sees its average, star spread and comments (without customer
names). Jamzo may hide abusive reviews.

## 7. Term and ending
This agreement starts when Jamzo makes the Restaurant live and continues until either side ends it with
[[15 days]]' written notice. Jamzo may suspend the Restaurant immediately for food-safety problems, fraud, or
repeated serious complaints. Money already earned is settled after ending, less any amounts owed.

## 8. Other terms
Each party keeps its own trademarks; the Restaurant lets Jamzo show its name, logo, photos and menu on Jamzo.
Indian law applies; courts at [[Mehsana, Gujarat]].
